ISUFST Mission & Vision

Iloilo State University of Fisheries Science and Technology

🔭

VISION

A leading and empowering research university in fisheries, agriculture, education, maritime studies, arts, sciences, and technology in Southeast Asia by 2030 (BOR Reso. No. 209, s. 2025).

🎯

MISSION

To produce globally competitive and empowered graduates in fisheries, agriculture, education, maritime studies, arts, sciences, and technology, and to capacitate individuals and communities towards sufficiency for nation building. (BOR Reso. No. 209, s. 2025).
